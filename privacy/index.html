Privacy

This site collects one thing, and only when you choose to give it: the email address you enter to join the reader list.

Reader list

When you join the reader list, your email address and optional first name are sent to MailerLite, the email service used by K.J. Crask. MailerLite stores that information and uses it to deliver the bonus scene you asked for, release news, and reader-only email. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing takes effect immediately.

What this site does not do

This site sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics scripts. It does not record your IP address, browser fingerprint, or browsing behaviour. It does not sell or share your information.

Bonus content and purchase links

The ROOFTOP bonus scene is delivered through BookFunnel, which applies its own privacy terms. Purchase links open Amazon, BookBub, or Goodreads, and those services process activity under their own privacy policies. This site does not receive your purchase details.

Questions and requests

To see, correct, or delete what is held about you, or for any other privacy question, email hello@kjcrask.com.
